## Win-Back Campaign

An always-on win-back campaign for lost customers at Veriforce (2025), built on an A/B test of re-engagement timing.

## The starting point

Power BI data suggested the best window to re-engage lost customers had historically been between 4 and 6 months. That was observed with no active campaigns running, so it had never been tested.

## The test

The data was stratified and customers were then randomly selected, to avoid bias. One group received no communications as the control. The test groups were contacted at 3 months (Pot A), 4 months (Pot B), 5 months (Pot C) and 6 months (Pot D).

## The result

Pot B, contacted at 4 months, was the clear winner, raising conversion to sale by 6% compared with the control.
