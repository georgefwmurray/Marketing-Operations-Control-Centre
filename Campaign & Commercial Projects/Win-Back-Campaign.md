## Overview

I developed the strategy and built the automation for a win-back campaign targeting former customers who had cancelled at least three months earlier.

The programme aimed to reintroduce the value of the service before using a joining-fee incentive, supported by social retargeting. Rather than leading with a discount, the journey first reminded customers why the product was worth returning to.

## Audience and approach

The audience was made up of cancelled customers at least three months beyond cancellation. The same campaign journey was used for all eligible customers.

A control group that did not receive the campaign provided the comparison for measuring the impact on conversion to sale.

## Campaign journey

The email sequence ran fortnightly, with three messages:

1. **Value reminder:** Reintroduced the benefits and value available to customers.
2. **Reinforcement:** Continued to communicate the reasons to return, without an incentive.
3. **Joining-fee offer:** Introduced a waived joining fee as the final step in the sequence.

Social retargeting ads supported the email activity, reinforcing the win-back message across paid social channels.

## My role and delivery

I defined the win-back strategy and personally built the campaign automation.

The Content Manager was responsible for the campaign communications, while the PPC Manager managed the retargeting ads. This brought together marketing automation, content and paid media under a coordinated campaign approach.

## Measurement and results

The campaign was evaluated against a control group that was not targeted.

The test group achieved a **6% uplift in conversion to sale** compared with the control group.

## Why it mattered

The campaign used a measured, value-first approach to customer reactivation. By reserving the waived joining-fee offer for the final email, it gave customers reasons to reconsider before introducing a financial incentive. The controlled comparison provided evidence of the additional conversion associated with the campaign.
