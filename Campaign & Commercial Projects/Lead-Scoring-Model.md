## Overview

A lead scoring model at Veriforce (2024) that lets sales prioritise higher-fit leads.

## How it works

- **Fit:** demographic and firmographic scoring, using enrichment against the ICP
- **Intent:** behaviour scoring, with higher-converting forms earning a higher score
- **Visibility:** the score is shown on the lead, so sales reps can work the higher-fit, higher-intent leads first
