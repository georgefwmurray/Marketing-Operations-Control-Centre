## Insurance Partner Integration

Aligned contact strategies with an insurance partner at Veriforce (2026) to remove duplicated outreach.

## The problem

Communications from Veriforce and the insurance partner overlapped, which duplicated outreach to the same audience.

## What changed

- Both sides agreed a joint contact strategy
- Retargeting ads became dual branded
- Emails during the journey became dual branded, which also helped reduce crossover

## Result

**17% decrease in email send volume**
