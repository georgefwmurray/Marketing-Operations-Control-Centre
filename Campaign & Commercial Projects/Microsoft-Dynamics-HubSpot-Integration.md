## Overview

A business-wide Microsoft Dynamics ↔ HubSpot integration at Veriforce (2024).

## What syncs

- Contact, account/company, lead and opportunity/deal data
- A subset of fields and properties that are vital for marketing segmentation and reporting
- Leads sync to a custom HubSpot object, which gives full control over how they are handled

## Activity and sales events

- HubSpot activity events, such as page views and form fills, sync across so sales can see all of a contact's marketing activity
- Sales events sync to HubSpot, so marketing can segment on contact rates and pull personalisation from within sales communications

## My role

Drove the HubSpot side of the integration, ensuring marketing data integrity and sales alignment.

## Result

**Average lead-to-sales handover time cut from 7 days to 4, a reduction of over 40%**
