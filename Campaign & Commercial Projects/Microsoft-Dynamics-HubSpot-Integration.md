<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>The business challenge</h2>
  <p>Marketing and Sales needed a more dependable connection between HubSpot and Microsoft Dynamics. Without the right data and activity moving between the platforms, teams had less visibility of the customer journey and the context needed to act on new leads.</p>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>Connecting the platforms</h2>
  <p>The integration connected the core records used by both teams, while keeping the HubSpot data model useful for marketing operations.</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Contact, account/company, lead and opportunity/deal data synchronised between the systems.</li>
    <li>A defined subset of fields and properties supported marketing segmentation and reporting.</li>
    <li>Leads synchronised to a custom HubSpot object, providing control over how lead records were managed.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>Making customer activity visible</h2>
  <p>HubSpot activity, including page views and form submissions, was made available in Dynamics so Sales could see relevant marketing engagement. Sales events also flowed back into HubSpot, allowing marketing to use contact-rate information for segmentation and to bring sales context into communications.</p>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>My role</h2>
  <p>I drove the HubSpot side of the integration, focusing on the marketing data model, data integrity and alignment with the Sales process. This meant ensuring the information synchronised was useful to marketing teams as well as accessible to Sales in their CRM.</p>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>The result</h2>
  <p><strong>Average lead-to-sales handover time reduced from seven days to four — a reduction of more than 40%.</strong></p>
  <p>The integration improved the flow of lead and engagement information between marketing and sales systems, helping teams act on leads sooner with better context.</p>
</section>
