## Turning account intelligence into a conversation

The ABM programme focused on 15 large target accounts. One priority account had no previous engagement despite attempts through conventional email and sales-call outreach.

The opportunity was to make account research and buying-group outreach more systematic, while keeping a person in control of which stakeholders were approached.

## The workflow

I owned the automation decisions and technical builds, connecting HubSpot, ZoomInfo and Lemlist into a repeatable process:

1. **HubSpot — lead creation:** A lead was created in HubSpot to initiate the account research workflow.
2. **ZoomInfo — account and stakeholder intelligence:** ZoomInfo researched the company, identified relevant buying-group stakeholders and matched those stakeholders against known HubSpot contacts.
3. **HubSpot — human review:** The ABM Manager reviewed and confirmed the proposed stakeholder list in HubSpot before any outreach began.
4. **Lemlist — targeted outreach:** The approved contacts were enrolled into Lemlist for LinkedIn InMail outreach.

This replaced a largely manual approach to account research, buying-group identification and outreach preparation. The approval step remained with the ABM Manager; my responsibility was the automation logic and technical delivery behind the process.

## The commercial result

**One large-account win within 12 weeks, generating approximately £40,000 in revenue.**

The account had previously shown no engagement despite other email and sales-call attempts. Lemlist LinkedIn InMail was the trigger that opened the conversation and created traction where those earlier approaches had failed.

The result came from a focused 15-account programme. It demonstrates how connected account intelligence, CRM data and carefully controlled outreach can help sales teams engage hard-to-reach prospects.

## My contribution

I was responsible for the automation decisions and technical builds across the workflow: defining how account research, stakeholder matching, approval and outreach should connect, then implementing the process across HubSpot, ZoomInfo and Lemlist.

The ABM Manager retained the decision to approve the stakeholder list before enrolment. This kept human judgement in the process while removing manual effort from the operational steps around it.
