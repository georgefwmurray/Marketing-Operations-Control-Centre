## ABM Strategy Automation

Owned all automation for a new ABM strategy at Veriforce (2026), targeting a list of 15 large accounts.

## What the automation covered

- Pulling key contacts in each account's buying group from ZoomInfo and Apollo
- Mapping those contacts in HubSpot and identifying gaps in coverage
- A Lemlist integration enabling multi-channel outreach through LinkedIn InMail and email

## Result

**1 large-account win within 12 weeks of launch**, from a 15-account target list
