<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>The business problem</h2>
  <p>Two issues were slowing the journey from initial enquiry to sale: making contact with leads quickly enough, and helping prospects understand the value of the product. Improving one part of the process in isolation would not address the whole journey.</p>
  <p>The programme therefore brought together lead qualification, marketing communications and Sales response into one coordinated approach.</p>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>One connected acceleration programme</h2>
  <h3>1. Identify the right leads</h3>
  <p>I owned the HubSpot lead-scoring work. The model combined several signals to distinguish genuine buying potential from low-intent activity:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Explicit intent:</strong> form submissions, with higher-intent forms carrying greater weight.</li>
    <li><strong>Ideal customer profile:</strong> fit against the target customer profile.</li>
    <li><strong>Engagement:</strong> email opens, replies and clicks, alongside website activity and page views.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>A score of 40 was the threshold for Sales qualification. Reaching the score initiated a further automated check of the contact's job title and industry. Only leads meeting those final fit criteria were passed to Sales. This combined behavioural intent with a final audience-fit check, rather than relying on a score alone.</p>

  <h3>2. Build product understanding</h3>
  <p>The wider Marketing team developed nurture emails to educate prospects and communicate product value. I designed the automation plan and coordinated this workstream with the scoring and Sales-response activity, so that leads received relevant communication as they progressed.</p>

  <h3>3. Improve speed to conversation</h3>
  <p>The programme incorporated the instant-callback process I designed and built using Response iQ, HubSpot, Zapier and Microsoft Dynamics. When a lead submitted a form, the workflow enabled a rapid callback and connected the answering agent to the lead-routing process. Sales sequences and structured follow-up provided a more consistent approach to continuing the conversation.</p>
  <p>This connected the qualification decision with a faster human response, reducing the gap between a prospect showing interest and Sales speaking to them.</p>
</section>

  <h2>My role</h2>

    <li>Designed the overall automation plan and coordinated the scoring, nurture and Sales follow-up workstreams.</li>
    <li>Personally designed and delivered the Response iQ technical integration, connecting HubSpot, Zapier and Microsoft Dynamics.</li>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>The result</h2>
  <p><strong>The lead-to-sale cycle reduced from 11 days to 7.5 days — a 32% reduction.</strong></p>
  <p>The improvement was seen across all lead sources and all brands, with a similar percentage reduction across the business. It was measured in a before-and-after comparison following full programme rollout, rather than being limited to one campaign or acquisition channel.</p>
  <div class="case-study-note"><strong>Measurement:</strong> The reported lead-to-sale cycle moved from 11 to 7.5 days after full rollout. The improvement was consistent across brands and lead sources; no separate channel-specific uplift is claimed.</div>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>Why it mattered</h2>
  <p>The programme addressed the whole path from enquiry to conversation: prioritising leads with a combination of intent and fit, helping prospects understand the product, and making Sales follow-up faster and more consistent. It brought together hands-on HubSpot automation, cross-platform integration and coordinated delivery across Marketing and Sales — with a measurable improvement across the business.</p>
</section>
