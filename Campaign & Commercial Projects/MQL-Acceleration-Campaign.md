## Overview

An always-on campaign at Veriforce (2026) targeting open leads and opportunities to accelerate their progression through the pipeline.

## How it works

- **New leads:** every newly open inbound lead receives the campaign as soon as it is created
- **Always on:** the emails keep sending for as long as the lead has not reached closed won
- **Opportunities:** once a lead becomes an opportunity, the communications switch to focused emails based on the opportunity stage
