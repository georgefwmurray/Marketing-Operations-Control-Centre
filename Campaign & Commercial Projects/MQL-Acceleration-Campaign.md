<p class="case-study-intro">An always-on campaign designed to maintain relevant communication with inbound leads and open opportunities as they progressed through the sales pipeline.</p>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>The business problem</h2>
  <p>New inbound leads and open opportunities needed timely, relevant follow-up while they progressed through different stages of the pipeline. A single generic nurture sequence would not reflect the change in context when a lead became an opportunity.</p>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>The approach</h2>
  <p>I launched an always-on campaign with communications that changed according to the lead's status:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>New inbound leads:</strong> entered the campaign when the lead was created.</li>
    <li><strong>Open leads:</strong> continued to receive the sequence while they had not reached closed won.</li>
    <li><strong>Opportunities:</strong> moved to focused communications based on the opportunity stage.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>This gave the programme a continuous presence while keeping the messaging aligned to the prospect's position in the pipeline.</p>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>My contribution</h2>
  <p>I launched the campaign and its stage-based approach to communication across new inbound leads and open opportunities.</p>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>The result</h2>
  <p><strong>The reported lead-to-sale cycle reduced from 11 days to 7.5 days — a 32% reduction.</strong></p>
  <div class="case-study-note"><strong>Measurement note:</strong> The available project record supports the change in lead-to-sale cycle time. It does not specify the comparison period, sample size or attribution methodology, so no additional causal or revenue claims are made here.</div>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>Why it mattered</h2>
  <p>The programme made follow-up more consistent and responsive to pipeline stage. It is an example of lifecycle automation being used to support sales progression rather than simply increasing email volume.</p>
</section>
