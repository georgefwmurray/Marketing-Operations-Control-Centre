<p class="case-study-intro">An always-on re-engagement programme that used historical booking patterns to reconnect with prospects who had previously been marked closed-lost.</p>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>The business problem</h2>
  <p>Closed-lost leads were not necessarily permanently lost. In a seasonal travel business, a prospect's timing and circumstances could change, and previous enquiry behaviour offered a useful signal for when to reconnect.</p>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>The insight</h2>
  <p>Power BI analysis showed that customers often enquired and booked holidays at a similar time each year. That pattern created an opportunity to time re-engagement around the period when a previous prospect was more likely to be considering a trip again.</p>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>The approach</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Segmented closed-lost leads by the reason they were lost and when the original lead was created.</li>
    <li>Used historical booking patterns to inform the timing of re-engagement.</li>
    <li>Combined tailored communications with social retargeting advertising.</li>
    <li>Kept the programme always on so that relevant prospects could be re-engaged over time.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>My contribution</h2>
  <p>I developed and launched the always-on campaign approach, using the available booking insight to shape segmentation, timing and tailored communications.</p>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>The result</h2>
  <p><strong>The campaign achieved 23% lead-to-won conversion and became the third-highest-converting lead source.</strong></p>
  <div class="case-study-note"><strong>Measurement note:</strong> The project record identifies the lead-to-won conversion rate and relative source ranking. It does not provide a comparison group or incremental revenue figure.</div>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>Why it mattered</h2>
  <p>The programme treated closed-lost records as a potential future audience rather than a permanently exhausted list. It combined CRM segmentation, historical insight and coordinated communications to create a repeatable source of converted business.</p>
</section>
