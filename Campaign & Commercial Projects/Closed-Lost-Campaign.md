# Closed-Lost Campaign

## Overview

An always-on, personalised campaign targeting closed-lost leads at Collett's Mountain Holidays (2023), re-engaging them with tailored messaging over time.

## How it works

- **Targeting:** closed-lost leads are segmented by closed reason and by when the original lead was created
- **Timing:** Power BI data showed that customers of this travel business tend to enquire and book holidays at the same time each year
- **Channels:** tailored communications plus social retargeting ads

## My role

Launched the campaign.

## Result

**23% lead-to-won conversion, the 3rd highest-converting lead source**
