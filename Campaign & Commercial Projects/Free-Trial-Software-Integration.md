<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>The business challenge</h2>
  <p>The self-serve free-trial journey needed to connect more directly with the CRM and Sales process. A sign-up on the platform needed to become a usable lead record in HubSpot, with the account reference required by Sales to identify and follow up the trial.</p>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>Connecting sign-up to lead management</h2>
  <p>I worked on the integration that connected the platform's free-trial sign-up to HubSpot through an API. The process was:</p>
  <ol>
    <li>A user registers for a free trial on the platform.</li>
    <li>The platform triggers an API call to HubSpot.</li>
    <li>HubSpot creates the corresponding lead record.</li>
    <li>The lead is made available to Sales with the platform account number attached.</li>
  </ol>
  <p>This removed the gap between the product sign-up and the CRM record Sales needed to work from.</p>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>The result</h2>
  <p><strong>Lead volume increased by 4% following the integration.</strong> The project connected product-led trial activity with the existing lead-management process, giving Sales a clearer route from sign-up to follow-up.</p>
</section>
