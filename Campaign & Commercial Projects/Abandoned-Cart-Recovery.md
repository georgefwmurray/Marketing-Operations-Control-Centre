## Abandoned Cart Recovery

The introduction of an abandoned cart recovery journey for Veriforce e-commerce (2025).

## The problem

The original journey was basic. When a lead abandoned their cart, a couple of emails were sent and that was it.

## What changed

- Abandoned carts now create leads in the CRM
- Those leads are handed over to sales
- The nurture journey was revised and now includes SMS alongside email

## My role

Led the introduction of the journey, working with technical stakeholders on the build. Owned the copywriting, email creation and send cadence.

## Result

**17% YoY uplift in e-commerce revenue**
