## The challenge

Collett’s Mountain Holidays relied on a bespoke CRM that had become a constraint on growth. Reporting was limited, marketing automation options were minimal, attribution was difficult and the platform was hard to scale.

The business needed a system that could support more effective campaign execution and nurture, while giving Marketing, Sales, Operations and Customer Service a shared platform for their work.

## My role

I identified HubSpot as the preferred platform following competitor analysis and took responsibility for the HubSpot workstream from evaluation through implementation and adoption.

I gathered requirements from teams across Marketing, Sales and Operations, then led the HubSpot-side design, configuration, process development and automation build. I also helped shape the data migration, deciding which information from the legacy CRM should be brought across.

The implementation connected HubSpot with the website, GA4 and the custom travel booking system. Most connections used native platform connectors; the booking-system integration required API calls, delivered in collaboration with the developers responsible for the other systems.

I also owned training and adoption across the business, including Sales, Marketing, Operations and Customer Service. The first teams were logging in six months after the initial competitor analysis. Training and adoption continued after launch.

## Making the platform useful

The change was more than a CRM replacement. HubSpot gave the business the ability to execute campaigns more consistently, build nurture programmes that the old system could not support and create repeatable processes across Marketing and Sales.

Sales Sequences also improved the consistency of follow-up, helping reduce the time from lead to sale. The estimated reduction was approximately 10%; this is a recalled estimate rather than a precisely verified figure.

## Results

- **125% year-on-year growth in MQLs** in the first full year after implementation, compared with the previous year.
- Improved sales conversion following the introduction of more consistent follow-up processes.
- Approximately **10% faster lead-to-sale time**, based on a recalled estimate.
- A shared HubSpot platform supporting Marketing, Sales, Operations and Customer Service.

## The outcome

Within six months, the business had moved from a restrictive, custom-built CRM to a scalable HubSpot Enterprise platform. The implementation created the foundations for stronger campaign execution, automated nurture and more coordinated work across departments, with a 125% increase in MQLs in the first full year.
