<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>The delivery challenge</h2>
  <p>A brand refresh needed to be reflected consistently across four websites. The work involved more than replacing a logo: visual assets, page content and brand positioning all needed to be reviewed and updated while keeping delivery organised across a broad set of pages.</p>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>How I approached it</h2>
  <ol>
    <li>Created an inventory of the website assets that needed to change.</li>
    <li>Mapped the affected pages across all four websites.</li>
    <li>Identified longer-tail work and prioritised it against the available timelines.</li>
    <li>Worked closely with the content team to coordinate copy and asset updates and maintain consistency.</li>
  </ol>
</section>

<section class="case-study-section">
  <h2>My contribution and outcome</h2>
  <p>I led the website-side planning and coordination, connecting the asset and page-level work with the content team. The result was a coordinated brand refresh delivered across four websites.</p>
  <p>This case study records the delivery scope. No conversion or revenue uplift is attributed to the rebrand because a directly comparable measure has not been established.</p>
</section>
