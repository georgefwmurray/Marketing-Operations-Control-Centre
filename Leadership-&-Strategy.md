<section class="content-section leadership-intro">
  <div class="leadership-statement">
    <p class="eyebrow">The mission</p>
    <h2>Move marketing operations from manual execution to an intelligent, governed operating function.</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="prose">
    <p>The Marketing Operations Control Centre is one expression of that ambition: a connected system in which agents handle repeatable checks, preparation and insight, while people retain accountability for decisions, quality and customer impact.</p>
    <p>The goal is not automation for its own sake. It is to give skilled people more time for strategic work, stakeholder partnership and the judgement calls that should remain human.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="content-section">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">Developing capability</p>
    <h2>Build capability in the team, not dependency on one person.</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="prose">
    <p>A sustainable operating model gives people ownership of the systems they help create. Team members are encouraged to identify repetitive work, propose agent ideas, refine them through review and take responsibility for continuous improvement.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Promoted an intern to Digital Operations Executive, who now owns the continuous development of the Data Import Agent.</li>
      <li>Created a team-led approach to agent development, with ideas coming from the people closest to the workflows.</li>
      <li><strong>HubSpot AI Mentor (2026):</strong> selected by HubSpot as a mentor on its AI and agents programme, alongside participation as a mentee.</li>
      <li><strong>AI Mentor, Veriforce (2026):</strong> mentored two North American colleagues through end-to-end AI builds, including a sales dashboard and a social-listening agent.</li>

<section class="content-section">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">AI governance and risk</p>
    <h2>Governance before scale. Human accountability by design.</h2>
    <p>Automation should make the operation more dependable, not create a new category of unmanaged risk.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="principle-grid">
    <article class="principle-card"><h3>Build, buy or partner deliberately</h3><p>Build where the workflow is specific to how the organisation operates; buy where a mature product solves a generic need more efficiently; partner where specialist risk or compliance expertise is needed.</p></article>
    <article class="principle-card"><h3>Make governance part of the architecture</h3><p>Every agent request is logged and checked for overlap. New builds require approval. Live agents have an owner, a risk tier, a defined failure mode, a rollback path and a scheduled review.</p></article>
    <article class="principle-card"><h3>Keep a human accountable</h3><p>Agents can prepare, check, monitor and recommend. People remain accountable for approvals, decisions and customer-facing outcomes.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>
