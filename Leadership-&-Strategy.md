<section class="content-section leadership-intro">
  <div class="leadership-statement">
    <p class="eyebrow">Leadership remit</p>
    <h2>Lead the function. Align the work. Build capability.</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="prose">
    <p>I lead a four-person Digital Marketing Operations team, including three direct reports, supporting a 25-person marketing organisation across five brands.</p>
    <p>My remit spans strategy, people leadership, marketing technology, digital platforms and performance. Working with the SVP Marketing and functional leaders, I translate commercial priorities into plans, guide investment decisions and build the operating discipline needed to deliver.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="content-section">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">Strategic marketing partnership</p>
    <h2>Connect commercial ambition to the marketing operating plan.</h2>
    <p>My role is to help senior marketing leaders turn business objectives into measurable priorities, challenge assumptions with evidence and keep execution connected to commercial performance.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="leadership-grid">
    <article class="leadership-card"><span class="outcome-number">01</span><h3>Commercial planning and performance</h3><p>Contribute to twice-yearly H1 and H2 planning. Starting with the overall target, I factor in seasonality and year-on-year performance, then help set monthly lead targets by function and source. I review leads, workable leads and revenue against target with senior leaders each day, highlighting where activity or priorities may need to change.</p></article>
    <article class="leadership-card"><span class="outcome-number">02</span><h3>Customer-led prioritisation</h3><p>Lifecycle mapping exposed email oversaturation in renewal communications. I worked with stakeholders to reduce email volume by 17%, creating more room for relevant, value-led communication. Click-through rates increased, with no negative impact on retention.</p></article>
    <article class="leadership-card"><span class="outcome-number">03</span><h3>Constructive challenge</h3><p>For a smaller growth brand, I recommended a segmented and tested rollout rather than a blanket product promotion. The more targeted approach delivered higher-than-usual open and click-through rates and the brand’s best campaign ROI of the quarter.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="content-section">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">MarTech portfolio and commercial ownership</p>
    <h2>Make the case for the right technology, not simply more technology.</h2>
    <p>I lead the research, evaluation and operational delivery behind the marketing technology portfolio. The SVP Marketing retains final investment approval; my responsibility is to develop sound recommendations and make sure decisions work in practice.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="leadership-grid">
    <article class="leadership-card"><span class="outcome-number">01</span><h3>Portfolio strategy and investment decisions</h3><p>I assess capability gaps and evaluate whether to adopt, build, consolidate or retire technology. For example, I identified an underused A/B testing and personalisation subscription whose functionality was already covered by in-house WordPress development. I recommended cancellation, secured approval and actioned the change, releasing budget for campaigns and PPC.</p></article>
    <article class="leadership-card"><span class="outcome-number">02</span><h3>Platform consolidation</h3><p>I led competitor analysis behind the recommendation to consolidate a brand’s marketing platform into the existing HubSpot environment. The assessment considered licence costs, internal expertise and the training required, with final investment approval from the SVP Marketing.</p></article>
    <article class="leadership-card"><span class="outcome-number">03</span><h3>Vendor and budget management</h3><p>I manage renewals and new contract discussions, negotiating pricing and terms ahead of final approval. Annual budgets are agreed during Q4 planning. When new requirements emerge, I first look for funding within the existing portfolio through reprioritisation, consolidation or subscription savings.</p></article>
    <article class="leadership-card"><span class="outcome-number">04</span><h3>Integration ownership and enterprise partnership</h3><p>I own requirements, solution design and delivery for integrations between marketing platforms. For organisation-wide integrations, I represent Marketing and work with the central teams leading wider requirements and delivery. IT and Legal retain responsibility for their respective security, compliance and legal controls.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="content-section">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">Developing capability</p>
    <h2>Build capability in the team, not dependency on one person.</h2>
    <p>Developing people means giving them the knowledge, confidence and ownership to take on more complex work and contribute beyond their original role.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="prose">
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Developing talent:</strong> Supported a colleague from an internship into a full-time Digital Operations Executive role. They now own the continuous development of the Data Import Agent and are a trusted reviewer of AI agent builds.</li>
      <li><strong>Building wider marketing capability:</strong> Helped develop HubSpot proficiency across the 25-person marketing function, enabling colleagues to create emails and straightforward automations themselves.</li>
      <li><strong>Responsible AI adoption:</strong> Trained colleagues on Copilot Studio and AI agents, helping them distinguish between problems that need a new agent and those already solved by existing platforms.</li>
      <li><strong>HubSpot Agents in Action mentor (2026):</strong> Selected through an application process to mentor others on HubSpot’s AI agents programme.</li>
      <li><strong>Veriforce AI Mentor (2026):</strong> Guided two North American colleagues through end-to-end builds, including a sales dashboard and a social-listening agent.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>The objective is a more capable marketing organisation, with greater ownership across the team and specialist capacity available for higher-value operational priorities.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="content-section">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">How I lead</p>
    <h2>Ownership with support. Autonomy with accountability.</h2>
    <p>I believe people do their best work when they are trusted to take responsibility, understand what they are working towards and know they have support when they need it.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="prose">
    <p>I delegate responsibility rather than simply allocate tasks. I assign projects around individual expertise while creating opportunities to stretch into new areas. I provide direction, remove blockers and support stakeholder conversations without becoming a bottleneck for routine decisions.</p>
    <p>Weekly one-to-ones, monthly objective reviews and short daily ClickUp stand-ups create a regular rhythm for progress, development and problem-solving. A central ClickUp workflow allocates incoming work according to skills and capacity, while dashboards make measurable objectives—such as lead generation—visible.</p>
    <p>When delivery falls short, I start by understanding why. The issue may be capability, capacity, unclear expectations or missing support. Accountability remains important, but the response should be proportionate and informed by the circumstances.</p>
    <h2>Build an operation that doesn't depend on its leader.</h2>
    <p>I want the team to run day-to-day responsibilities without constant intervention. My role is to maintain strategic direction, remove obstacles, support stakeholder relationships and keep the function aligned with wider business priorities. That creates a more resilient team and gives people the space to own their work.</p>
  </div>
</section>
