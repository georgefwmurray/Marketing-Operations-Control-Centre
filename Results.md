  <h1>Better operations. More room for higher-value work.</h1>
  <p class="page-lead">For a B2B SaaS business across five brands, the Marketing Operations Control Centre brought quality assurance, production support, data operations, performance insight and governance into one connected operating model.</p>

<section class="content-section">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">Commercial impact</p>
    <h2>External content spend cut by 50%.</h2>
    <p>Reworked the balance between internal capability and external production, reducing recurring external content spend by half while maintaining a structured approach to quality and delivery.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="content-section">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">Operational performance</p>
    <h2>Changes in how marketing work gets done</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="outcome-grid">
    <article class="outcome-card"><span class="outcome-number">01</span><h3>More capacity for higher-value work</h3><p>Repeatable preparation, quality checks and reporting are handled more consistently, giving specialists greater scope to focus on analysis, planning and improvement.</p></article>
    <article class="outcome-card"><span class="outcome-number">02</span><h3>More consistent quality</h3><p>Common standards make campaign and website checks more repeatable, with issues surfaced before work reaches customers.</p></article>
    <article class="outcome-card"><span class="outcome-number">03</span><h3>Stronger operational governance</h3><p>Clear business purpose, accountability, documentation and human oversight are built into the way automation is introduced and managed.</p></article>
    <article class="outcome-card"><span class="outcome-number">04</span><h3>Better use of external support</h3><p>Internal capability and clearer processes reduced reliance on external content production without treating cost reduction as the only measure of success.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="content-section">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">How value is assessed</p>
    <h2>Separate cash impact from operational value.</h2>
    <p>The approach distinguishes external spend reduction from capacity released, quality improvements and risk reduction. These benefits are not combined into a single savings figure: each should be assessed against its own evidence and baseline.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="content-section">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">The wider lesson</p>
    <h2>Measure what changes, not just what gets automated.</h2>
    <p>The value of an operating model is not the number of automations it contains. It is whether the function becomes more effective: better work, clearer accountability, more useful insight and more time for people to focus on decisions that need judgement.</p>
  </div>
</section>
